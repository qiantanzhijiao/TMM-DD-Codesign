without re-running SCAPS. The SCAPS executable is closed-source software and
is NOT redistributed in the source archive, for licensing reasons. All
inputs, scripts, native SCAPS outputs and diagnostic logs needed to audit the

Reproducibility note
--------------------
The comparison criteria were fixed before the SCAPS run and were not revised
afterwards. The machine-readable verdict is acceptance_verdict.json:
2 PASS / 2 FAIL, all_pass = false. Two of the four criteria involve
quantities that are not comparable by construction (UniDD Dirichlet carrier
contacts versus SCAPS flat-band / surface-recombination contacts;
wavelength-integrated imported G(z) versus SCAPS internal spectral QE).
These FAIL verdicts are retained verbatim as pre-specified outcomes; they are
reported as model-mismatch diagnostics and are not a validation claim.
